# Provenance

This repository was rewritten from reporting problems encountered while building and reviewing private voice/booking analytics.

## Preserved

- named denominators;
- measured/modelled/unavailable basis labels;
- refusal of unsupported attribution;
- explicit limitations attached to the report.

## Rewritten for public review

Only synthetic call records are used. Private analytics data, customer records, and operational dashboards are excluded.

## Claim boundary

The examples demonstrate reporting rules. They are not production metrics and should not be read as customer or revenue results.
