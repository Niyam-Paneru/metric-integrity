# Invariants

1. Every rate carries a named population plus numerator and denominator counts.
2. Evidence basis and quote permission are separate decisions.
3. Modelled does not become measured through arithmetic or formatting.
4. `unavailable` carries no numeric value.
5. Unsupported attribution names the missing evidence and stays unavailable.
6. Zero-denominator rates are unavailable, not zero.
7. Only measured figures explicitly allowed for the intended claim are safe to quote.
